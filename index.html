      💚 What Actually Works — Prevention Is Possible
      🔒

      Means Restriction

      • Ban/safeguard lethal pesticides
      • Bridge barriers & firearm safety
      • Package-limited medicines
      Evidence: Sri Lanka cut suicides ~70% after banning highly lethal pesticides — the best-documented national success.
      🏫

      Schools As Safety Nets

      • Free counselors in secondary schools
      • Lower one-shot-exam stakes
      • Gatekeeper training for teachers
      Evidence: Finland cut once-high rates via school health services; Philippines' 2018 Mental Health Act mandates school programs.
      📞

      Helplines & Gatekeepers

      • 24/7 crisis lines with real capacity
      • Peer & community gatekeepers
      • Follow-up after attempts
      Evidence: France's 3114 line + follow-up care are WHO-listed core strategies.
      ⚖️

      Law & Services

      • Decriminalize attempted suicide (India did, 2017)
      • Train general doctors in mental health
      • Responsible media reporting
      Evidence: Most low-income countries have <1 psychiatrist per 100,000 people — task-shifting to trained general workers is the proven fix.
